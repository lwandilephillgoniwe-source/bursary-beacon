## Goal of Phase 2
Start earning (ads + affiliate), and expand coverage and site structure so traffic can
compound. Never at the cost of quality — thin pages get us penalised.

## Tasks

**1. Trust pages + analytics plumbing (do this before applying for ads).**
AdSense needs a genuinely useful, trustworthy site, and a brand-new domain with a handful of
AI-enriched pages is a real rejection risk. So sequence it carefully:
- Build the **trust pages** first: About, Contact, Editorial policy, **AI usage policy**
  (plainly state what AI does and doesn't do), Privacy/POPIA.
- Wire up **Google Search Console** AND **GA4** now, so we can see indexing *and* real
  visitor behaviour (bounce, pages/session) — not just whether Google sees us.
- **Do not apply for AdSense yet.** Let the site get indexed and settle first, and aim to
  apply once we have roughly **40–50 genuinely strong published pages**, not 20–30. Ads on a
  zero-traffic site earn nothing anyway, so there's no cost to waiting and a real cost to
  applying too early (rejection is harder to recover from than delay).

**2. AdSense ad units (once approved).**
- Add ad units placed tastefully — never so many that they hurt readability or page
  experience. Do not add ads to thin or draft pages.
- REALITY CHECK to hold in mind: our audience is low-income, mobile, often zero-rated. RPMs
  will be low. Treat ads as table-stakes income, not the business — the real money later is
  the education-provider layer, not display ads.

**3. Affiliate / referral links.**
Add a system for placing **helpful** affiliate/referral links where they genuinely fit —
e.g. CV-writing services, online courses, laptop deals — near "required documents" or "how
to apply" sections. Keep them clearly relevant and not spammy. Make link placement
configurable so I can manage partners.

**4. Grow bursary coverage.**
- Add more Tier-1 bursary collectors, prioritising **high-search-demand named bursaries**
  (the ones people actually search for) over obscure ones.
- Keep the quality gate strict — publish more only by publishing *better*, never thin.

**5. Hub pages + internal linking.**
- Build **category hubs** (e.g. by field of study) and **province hubs** that list and link
  to their opportunities.
- Build **organisation profile pages** (one hub per funder/company, linking all its
  opportunities).
- On every opportunity page, link to genuinely related opportunities (same type/field/
  province).
- This spreads crawl equity, builds topical authority, and keeps users on-site.

**6. Search Console + sitemap (finish the SEO plumbing).**
- Ensure the auto-updating XML sitemap adds each new page on publish and is submitted to
  Google Search Console.
- Confirm robots.txt allows published pages and blocks admin/draft.
- Expired pages: keep indexed with a clear "Closed" status OR noindex — do not delete
  (no dead links).

**7. Performance + mobile pass.**
- Strong Core Web Vitals: fast load, low layout shift.
- Correctly sized/compressed images with width/height set; lazy-load only below-the-fold
  images (never the main content).
- Fully responsive, mobile-first — most SA traffic is mobile.
